# Strength of Evoking: the kids' guide

<!-- organize-docs: pinned -->

This is the short, easy version. It tells you the main things the spell does. For every rule and number,
see the full guide: [`evoking-functional-spec.md`](evoking-functional-spec.md).

## What is it?

An evoker is the wizard illager that lives in woodland mansions. It makes fangs pop out of the ground and
calls little flying helpers called vexes.

**Strength of Evoking** is a spell that lets *you* do that.

## Turning it on and off

- Read a **Scroll of Evoking** to turn the spell on. It never runs out.
- Sneak and read a scroll to turn it off. Drinking milk turns it off too.
- If you die, the spell is gone when you come back.

## The keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Y** | Fangs |
| **V** | Call vexes |
| **J** and **K** | Pick and start a team move (only if the server is set to manual) |

When you press Y or V, your arms go up like an evoker's. One second later the magic happens. Then you
have to wait a bit before you can do it again: 5 seconds for fangs, 17 seconds for vexes.

## Fangs (Y)

Fangs come out of the ground under every enemy in the big square in front of you, and bite them.

- If an enemy steps away in time, one more fang chases it.
- Fangs never bite you or your friends.
- If there are no enemies, nothing happens and you see "No targets in range".

## Who are your friends?

Your spells and your vexes never hurt:

- you and your team
- your pets
- your vexes
- villagers and wandering traders
- iron golems (unless one is mad at you)

If a golem does get mad at you, your side can never kill it. When it gets low on health it calms down and
is your friend again.

## Bound Vexes (V)

Press V to call three **Bound Vexes**. They are your own vexes, and they stay with you forever. You can
have up to 12.

Your vexes:

- follow you everywhere, even through portals
- fight monsters that come close
- fight anything that hurts you, and anything you hit
- help your wolves and golems in a fight (and your wolves help them)
- fly away from a creeper that is about to blow up
- stay closer to you when it is dangerous: at night, in the Nether, and closest of all in the End

**Telling a vex to stay:** sneak and right-click it with an empty hand. It will guard that spot. Do it
again and it follows you.

## Giving your vexes gear

Right-click a vex while holding something, and it takes it. It hands back whatever it was holding before.

| Give it | What happens |
|---|---|
| Helmet, chestplate or leggings | It wears them. You can see the armor on it. |
| A better sword, an axe or a mace | It hits harder. |
| A bow or crossbow | It stays back and shoots arrows. |
| A harming potion | It throws it at enemies. |
| A healing potion | It becomes a healer and throws it at hurt friends. |
| A shield | It blocks hits from the front. |

Good news: nothing a vex holds ever wears out or gets used up. If a vex dies, it drops everything you
gave it.

To take a vex's weapon back, right-click it with an empty hand (without sneaking).

## Helpful things vexes do by themselves

- **Fix iron golems.** A vex taps a hurt golem with an iron ingot until it is healed.
- **Cure zombie villagers.** A vex never fights a zombie villager. It feeds it a golden apple and throws a
  potion. After 20 seconds of green and white smoke, the zombie is a villager again. It costs you nothing,
  and the villager gives you cheaper trades.
- **Bribe enemy vexes.** When an evoker's vex attacks you, one of your vexes holds out an emerald. Most of
  the time the enemy vex takes it and flies away for good.

## Rescues

- **Lava:** a vex pulls you toward dry ground. In the Nether it also puts out the fire on you.
- **Drowning:** a vex pushes you up to the air.
- **Ghast fireballs (Nether):** a vex knocks the fireball right back at the ghast.
- **The End:** your vexes break the dragon's crystals, and their hits do three times the damage.
- **Falling into the void (End):** a vex catches you, puts you back on the island and feeds you an
  enchanted golden apple.

## Team moves

When you have enough vexes, they work as a team. They pull out special gear, and it disappears when the
move is over.

| Move | When it happens | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| **Shield** | A big monster comes close | They grab shields and take turns bashing it back. |
| **Multishot** | A strong monster attacks one of your vexes | Three vexes fire three arrows at once. |
| **Evader** | You or a friend is badly hurt | Up to three vexes circle the hurt one, shove monsters away and knock arrows back. |
| **Raid** | A giant shows up (a Ravager, a Warden, the Wither or the Dragon) and you have all 12 vexes | They put on the best armor, line up, and charge together. Some fire rockets. |

You will see a message like "Your vexes form a Shield!" when a move starts.

## Casters

About one vex in three is a **Caster**. You can tell because it wears a torn purple robe. A Caster does
not fight. It casts two spells on you when you need them:

- **Ghostrail** when you are almost dead or stuck inside a wall.
- **Mirror** when a few monsters are close.

## Mirror

Mirror makes **13 copies of you**. They walk around near you. When a monster picks you to attack, it
almost always goes after a copy by mistake. The copies cannot fight, and they poof away when they are hit
enough or when the spell ends. It lasts 6 minutes.

## Ghostrail

Ghostrail turns you into a kind of ghost for 4 minutes:

- you can fly
- you can go right through walls
- nothing can hurt you (but do not fall out of the world!)
- you leave a trail of white smoke

When it ends inside a wall, the game moves you to an open spot nearby.

You can read Mirror and Ghostrail scrolls yourself, too.

## Illagers with scrolls

Some evokers, vindicators and pillagers carry a Mirror or Ghostrail scroll. They use it when they first
see you: an evoker might make 13 copies of itself, or a pillager might fly through a wall at you. If you
beat one, it may drop the scroll.

## Villager ghosts

When a monster kills a villager, the villager leaves a **ghost** behind.

Later, if you have Strength of Evoking and you are in trouble near that spot, the ghost floats over to
you. It looks like the villager, but pale and see-through. It casts Mirror or Ghostrail on you to help.
When the fight is over it fades away. It can come back the next time you need it.
